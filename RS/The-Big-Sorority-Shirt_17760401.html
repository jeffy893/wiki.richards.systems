Richards Systems : The Big Sorority Shirt

Created by Jefferson Richards, last modified on Dec 06, 2025

Tammy is a sorority girl who wears a big shirt with greek letters, every day, and a little black or red dress on the weekend nights.

Brumhilda is a band girl who wears band t-shirts every day.

One day, both Tammy and Brumhilda each wear Insane Clown Posse shirts.

Tammy’s friends all chide her because she has no idiosyncrasy credit.

Brumhilda has all the idiosyncrasy credit, for this behavior of wearing shirts, so no one thinks twice about her outfit.
