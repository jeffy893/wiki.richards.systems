The Thesis

Optionality and loyalty are not real without each other.

MANGOS (Maieutic and Applicable Negotiations, Guarantees, and Opportunities) is a framework for interdependent sovereignty among families and local communities. It rejects top-down value imposition and instead builds antifragile, localized structures within a cognitively manageable group — bounded by Dunbar's Number (~150 people).

The strategy operates on three levels:

  1. Constitutional — Define the boundaries of reality so that betrayal has no structural opportunity to exist. Make loyalty authentic through contracts, bylaws, and mutual accountability.

  2. Charter — Translate insurance exclusions and financial risks into behavioral bylaws. The Family Charter is the Put Option on Loyalty — it protects the downside risk of human behavior.

  3. Operational — Run the risks of entrepreneurial life with a barbell strategy: extreme safety in the core, calculated risk at the margins. Skin in the game always.

Capital Model

MANGOS defines three forms of capital, each with its own balance-sheet logic:

  • Material Capital — Assets = Liabilities + Common Stock + Retained Earnings + Revenue − Expenses − Dividends

  • Social Capital — Social Power = Social Responsibilities + Common Social Stock + Previous Material Baggage + Exfluences − Influences − Takeables

  • Scope Capital — Scope Responsibilities = Scope Power (Focus) + Common Scope Stock + Previous Immaterial Baggage + Educateables − Ignoreables − Shareables

The quarterly 10Q report quantifies all three.

Anti-Fragility Principles (Article VI)

  • Prefer optionality over prediction

  • Small, reversible bets over large, irreversible ones

  • Barbell strategy: extreme safety in the core, calculated risk at the margins

  • Skin in the game: do not advise others to take risks you yourself would not take

  • Prepare for both shareholder economies and stakeholder economies
