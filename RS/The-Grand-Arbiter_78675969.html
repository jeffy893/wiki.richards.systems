Richards Systems : The Grand Arbiter

Samantha:

“Have you heard the news?! The fusion reactor competition has been won!”

Tim:

“Don’t bug me“

Samantha:

“How can this not matter to you?“

Tim:

“I am focused on the gulf… I am focused on the Grand Arbiter“

Samantha:

“Explain“

Tim:

“That a single LLM could retrospectively predict the outcome of court cases, across legal systems: from Sharia, to Sanskrit, to Mandarin, to English,…“

Samantha:

“well… I mean… how about we use the same competitive structure as the fusion reactors? harness greed by shooting for mars to land on the moon?“

Tim:

“Explain“

Samantha:

“Each country that can afford: trains on their legal system… and as they hone in on the target, they cross-compare with other legal systems…“

Tim:

[Perks Up… then slumps…]

“That garage laboratories per capita have access to cadavers… not dependent on John Hopkins… …“

Samantha:

“You’re just a grump, Tim… let’s go sing karaoke“

Tim:

[Perks up]

“Aight“
