Richards Systems : Crypto Mine

Created by Jefferson Richards, last modified on Dec 06, 2025

2017

Thus spake the pyramid:

"drive nails not screws -

I am your screw."

And the Fathers of this land spoke in unison:

"Do not upplant us."

And the pyramid asked:

"What of you are pitchers?"

And the Mothers chimed in:

"A pitcher is but the arm of an architect,

but we have no architects in this land."

So the pyramid turned over in conception.

Then spake the pyramid:

"I would not wrench you from your place

unless you were nails -

and this is what I want."

And the Men replied:

"How can we pitch

unless we are planted?"

And the Women responded:

"How can we architect

while we are conceiving?"

So the pyramid bore children.
